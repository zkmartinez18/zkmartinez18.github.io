Cybersecurity Protection Tools

Firewall

A firewall monitors network traffic and blocks unauthorized connections.

Antivirus

Antivirus software detects and removes malicious programs from devices.

Encryption

Encryption protects sensitive information by converting it into unreadable data.

Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA adds another layer of security by requiring multiple forms of verification.

VPN

A VPN creates an encrypted connection that helps protect data in transit.

Password Manager

A password manager securely stores passwords and helps users create strong, unique passwords for their online accounts.
