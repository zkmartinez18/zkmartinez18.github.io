Website Evaluations

Steam

Steam is an online gaming platform where users can purchase, download, and play video games. Its website has an organized layout that makes browsing games and finding information easy.

  • Contrast: Steam uses a dark blue background with lighter text, making information easy to read. Colorful game images also stand out against the background.
  • Repetition: The website consistently uses similar colors, fonts, and layouts throughout its pages. This makes the design feel organized.
  • Alignment: Games, navigation menus, and categories are arranged in organized rows and columns. This makes the website easier to navigate.
  • Proximity: Related games are grouped into categories such as featured games, special offers, and recommendations.

Heaven's Gate

Heaven's Gate is a website associated with the religious group of the same name. The website has an older design with bright colors, centered text, and large amounts of information. Its layout does not follow many modern web design practices.

  • Contrast: The website uses bright colors that can be distracting. Some of the color combinations make the content less comfortable to read.
  • Repetition: Although certain colors and text styles repeat, the overall design lacks consistency compared to modern websites.
  • Alignment: Much of the content is centered, and the large blocks of text make the website difficult to scan.
  • Proximity: Numerous links and large sections of text appear close together, making it difficult to distinguish related information.
